We help you look before you bid.

BankAuctions.ai exists so that an ordinary family can buy a bank-auctioned home with its eyes open.

Who we are

We are a small team of property people, lawyers, engineers and analysts based in South India. Most of us have sat across the table from someone who bid on a property and found out too late what they had bought. We started BankAuctions.ai because that should not keep happening.

What we are trying to do

Banks sell properties at a discount to recover their dues. The discount is real, but so are the risks, and the notice in the newspaper tells you very little. Our aim is simple: when you place a bid, you should know the property almost as well as the owner did. We are not an auctioneer, an agent or a bank, so we have nothing to gain from pushing you towards any particular property.

How we help you decide before you buy

Every listing starts with the bank notice, which we rewrite in plain words: the reserve price, the earnest money, the date, what the bank says about possession. Then we go further. A person from our team visits the property and photographs it as it is today, not as it was when the bank last looked. We check the title papers, the encumbrance certificate and the dues, and we put the guideline value, market value and a fair value next to the reserve price so you can see how big the discount really is.

Every way we use to tell you about a property

Photographs and short videos from the site visit. A map and the landmarks nearby. A written note on what is good and what to worry about, including tenants, litigation, dues and encroachments. The documents themselves, where we are allowed to share them. For the properties that matter most to you, a comprehensive report that we email within 72 hours. And if you would rather talk, you can speak to your Area Sales Manager or book a consultation with an empanelled advocate.

What we offer

Free search across banks in Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Kochi, Coimbatore and other South Indian cities. Pro packs that open the full file on the properties you shortlist. Premium properties with extra checks. Comprehensive reports. Legal consultations. A concierge who stays with you after you win, through the sale certificate, stamp duty, registration and possession. Property management and NRI services for those who live elsewhere.

How we look after our customers

Each property you unlock has a named Area Sales Manager, a real person with a phone number and an email address, who looks after your district. When you pay for something, he hears about it the same minute and gets in touch with you. If we say a property will open within 48 hours, we mean it, and if we are running late we tell you why. You can write to us or call us, and a person answers.
