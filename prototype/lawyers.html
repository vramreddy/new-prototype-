What a consultation is, and is not

The advocate advises you in their own professional capacity. BankAuctions.ai collects the fee and provides the papers on record, and takes no share of any further engagement you agree with them. A thirty-minute call is not a title certificate; ask the advocate for a separate scope for a full title search and encumbrance opinion.
