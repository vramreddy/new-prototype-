BankAuctions Field

The Android app for field verification engineers. This is a working prototype: sign in with a field engineer’s mobile number and the demo OTP.

  1. Open this page in Chrome on an Android phone.
  2. Tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. The app opens full screen with its own icon.

Everything you do here is shared with the website: reports go to the checker queue, and late visits show on the manager, Admin and Leadership dashboards.
