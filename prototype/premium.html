Why buyers pay for premium

A property is listed here only when it meets at least one of these, verified on site.

◩

Corner plots

Two road faces, better light, visibility and access.

✚

Major intersections

On or beside a busy junction where footfall and traffic never stop.

★

Next to major landmarks

Tech parks, airports, malls, stations and campuses within easy reach.

₹

Good rental yield

Rents in the pocket comfortably support the reserve price.

↗

Good resale value

Deep buyer demand so you can exit when you want.

What we screen out

We do not list properties near any of these, however cheap the reserve.

✕ Railway lines✕ Dumping yards✕ Burial grounds✕ Marriage halls✕ Temples and mosques✕ Drains and sewage canals✕ High-tension lines

How it works

  1. You pay the premium price for its category (₹1,180 to ₹4,720, GST included) for the property you choose.
  2. We do the background check and visit the property. Latest EC and papers from the bank, and our own site visit photographs and videos.
  3. We verify everything. Then we enable your features and confirm by email, WhatsApp and SMS, all within 48 hours.

Your unlock includes every Pro feature for that property, including an advocate’s time reserved with payment.
