Questions people ask us.

Things people ask us most often, in plain words.

What is the comprehensive property report?

For ₹8,850 including GST you get one consolidated report on a property, emailed to you within 72 hours of payment. It brings together our actual site visit findings, the valuation report, the encumbrance certificate since 1974, the bank’s legal opinion, photographs and videos, an advocate’s legal opinion, ground level valuation, nearest amenities, critical risks, guideline value, market value and fair value after ground level verification. Order it from any property page.

Are you an auctioneer or agent?

No. We provide intelligence and data on auction properties and prices. Bids are placed with the bank or on its e-auction portal, and we do not act as an intermediary.

What does the Free plan include?

You can browse properties and share them on social media. You also see the market price and nearby landmarks. Bank supplied pictures and documents, locality, lender, EC, fair value, AI ranking, our own site visit photographs and videos, and Talk to a lawyer are locked.

How do Pro packs work?

A Pro pack covers 20 properties of one category. Residential is ₹2,360, agricultural ₹2,950, industrial ₹5,900 and commercial ₹8,850, all including GST. Other property types are priced like industrial. Each unlocked property opens everything above, within 48 hours of payment.

What is the 48-hour promise?

After you pay, we do a fresh background check, visit the property, collect the latest EC and documents from the bank and verify everything. Your features open within 48 hours and we confirm by email, WhatsApp and SMS.

What are premium properties?

Corner plots, major intersections, properties beside major landmarks, and those with good rental yield or resale value. We never list properties near railway lines, dumping yards, burial grounds, marriage halls, temples, mosques, drains or sewage canals or high-tension lines. Each one unlocks on its own, with every Pro feature for it: ₹1,180 residential, ₹2,360 agricultural, ₹3,540 industrial or ₹4,720 commercial, GST included.

What is the AI Rank?

Each listing gets a score out of 100 from discount to market (up to 40), possession status (25), location and liquidity (20), and documents and photos (15). It is ranked only within its own category and city. The ranking is shown on unlocked properties.

Where do the photographs come from?

Some listings carry photographs released by the bank, marked Bank supplied. Our own site visit photographs and videos appear below the bank pictures on unlocked properties.

Can I share a property with someone?

Yes. Every user on every plan can share a property from its page by Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, X or email.

How do I talk to a lawyer?

On Pro and Premium, reserve an advocate’s time and pay the advocate’s fee at booking. The Free plan shows the option locked.

What is Concierge?

Help for successful bidders with registering the property. The fee is 2% of the sale amount. Property management (tenants, leases, rent collection, maintenance) and NRI services are separate and priced after we understand your needs. See the Concierge, Property management and NRI services pages.

Which cities do you cover?

Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Coimbatore and Kerala today. More cities are planned.

Does my Pro pack expire?

No. A pack ends only when you have unlocked all its properties, and every unlocked property stays in My archive.
